# Morrow theme background studies — v1

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These assets are enabled as the default backgrounds for their corresponding themes, at fixed 80% opacity for the app and 50% for chat. User-uploaded app and chat backgrounds independently override their defaults and retain their saved opacity settings; clearing an upload restores the current theme image. Night mode dims only built-in images through a brightness/saturation filter, retaining the respective opacity. Authentication is unchanged. Opaque portrait compositions with pale central negative space and soft watercolor details at the edges; no lettering or interface elements are baked in.

## Final generation prompts

### afternoon

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production bitmap theme wallpaper for a quiet, Chinese-first AI companionship web app, NOT a UI mockup.
Style/medium: restrained editorial watercolor and soft gouache on fine matte stationery paper, gentle naturalistic domestic details, elegant adult warmth rather than childish cuteness. Delicate visible brushwork, no hard outlines, low contrast.
Composition/framing: single portrait image, approximately 2:3 aspect ratio. At least 75% of the canvas is pale, near-uniform negative space, especially the entire central 65% width from top to bottom, for dark UI text and cards. Small identifiable motifs only at the outer margins and corners, softly fading inward. Do not put an object or a horizon across the center. Keep composition usable when cropped for a narrow phone or wider desktop. This must be a subtle real wallpaper, not a blank flat color and not a full room scene.
Constraints: no people, animals, stars, ocean, houses, text, handwriting, UI, logos, borders, frames, collage, watermarks, dramatic dark shadows, busy wallpaper repeats, or conspicuous grain. Opaque background.
Primary request: 午后 / Afternoon, warm cream-yellow theme. An airy sliver of translucent linen curtain catches diffused afternoon window light at the upper outer edge. A small, softly painted ivory ceramic cup is partially cropped into the lower outer corner. Only faint broad diagonal window-light washes, fading before reaching the center. Palette: warm ivory, pale wheat, muted honey, chalk cream; no saturated orange. Mood: a quiet afternoon with someone expected home.
```
